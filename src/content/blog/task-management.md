Recently, I created a frontend task management application focused on keeping everyday planning simple, visual, and easy to update.

The app is available here:

[Open the task management app](/apps/tasks)

The main idea is to have a lightweight interface for organizing work without a lot of ceremony. It is meant to be fast to use and easy to understand at a glance.

Some of the areas this app explores are:

- organizing tasks visually
- tracking progress through a simple interface
- keeping the workflow entirely in the frontend

If you would like to try it out, follow the link above and explore the app directly.
